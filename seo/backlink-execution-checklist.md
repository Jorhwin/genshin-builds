### N1 · Reddit 方案 A（Neuvillette）— 直接复制
```
For Neuvillette the main thing is that he wants to be the on-field carry, so the other three slots are all about keeping his Charged Attack uptime.

The premium comp is Neuvillette + Furina + Kazuha + Baizhu — Furina's fanfare stacks off his self-heal, Kazuha shreds Hydro RES, Baizhu keeps interruption resistance up during the CA channel.

If you don't have Furina, Zhongli works fine (shield > heal for CA uptime), and Xilonen can replace Kazuha for the shred.

I keep a full variant breakdown here if it helps: https://gameup.lol/team/neuvillette/
```

### C1 · Reddit 方案 A（Clorinde）— 直接复制
```
Clorinde works best in a team that lets her drive reactions without stealing her field time. Three common setups:

- Aggravate: Clorinde + Fischl (or Ororon) + Nahida + flex (Kazuha/Xilonen for grouping & shred). Fischl keeps field electro up, Nahida adds spread.
- Chevreuse Overload: Clorinde + Chevreuse + Xiangling + Fischl/Thoma. Chevreuse's kit buffs Overload teams hard and provides healing, so you drop a separate healer.
- If you lack Chevreuse, Aggravate is the simpler pickup; if you lack Nahida, Kirara still enables spread.

I keep the slot-by-slot replacement breakdown here: https://gameup.lol/team/clorinde/
```

### Navia · Reddit 方案 A — 直接复制
```
Navia wants Geo resonance + fast crystal generation. The standard is Navia + 2 Geo + 1 flex:

- Plunge variant: Navia + Xianyun + Bennett + second Geo (Zhongli/Albedo) — Xianyun turns her skill into plunge nukes.
- Standard: Navia + Xiangling + Bennett + Zhongli — Xiangling's mines + Bennett buffs, Zhongli shields & shreds.
- No Xianyun? The Xiangling/Bennett version is the most accessible.

Slot replacement detail: https://gameup.lol/team/navia/
```

### Arlecchino · Reddit 方案 A — 直接复制
```
Arlecchino is a self-sufficient on-field Pyro DPS; her teams enable her and keep her alive during the mask duration.

- Mono-Pyro: Arlecchino + Bennett + Kazuha + Xilonen/Zhongli — Kazuha shreds Pyro RES, Xilonen adds res shred + healing.
- Vaporize: Arlecchino + Yelan/Xingqiu + Zhongli + Bennett — higher ceiling, tighter rotation.
- Lack Xilonen? Zhongli fills shred + shield. Lack Kazuha? Sucrose does the VV shred.

Full variants + slot swaps: https://gameup.lol/team/arlecchino/
```
